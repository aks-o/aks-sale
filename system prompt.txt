Below is a practical, “no‑coding”, simple automation setup you can actually implement to sell:

- small tools
- software
- websites
- mining & crushing platform (industrial / high-ticket offer)

I’ll give you one **recommended stack**, plus a **clear workflow** you can follow.

---

## 1. What you really need (at minimum)

For your situation, the simplest effective automation system needs just three layers:

1. **Lead capture** – where interested people give you their info.
2. **CRM + pipeline** – where you track who is interested in what.
3. **Automation** – automatically send emails, assign tasks, and move deals along.

You don’t need 20 tools. One smart combination is enough.

---

## 2. Recommended “best simple” stack

### A. Lead capture: simple website or landing page

Use any simple builder you like, for example:

- A one‑page site on **Carrd**, **WordPress**, or **Systeme.io** (Systeme.io has a free plan with sales funnels and basic automation [1][2]).
- Each page/form should include:
  - Name
  - Email
  - Phone (especially for mining platform leads)
  - **Dropdown: “What are you interested in?”**
    - Tools
    - Software
    - Websites
    - Mining & crushing platform

That last field is critical – it lets you run different automation for each product type.

---

### B. CRM: free & simple

Use a CRM that is:

- free or cheap
- easy to use
- good at basic automation

You have two strong, simple options:

1. **HubSpot Free CRM**  
   - Free forever, basic CRM, email tracking, meeting scheduling, and simple automations [3][4].  
   - Good for starting out and centralizing contacts and deals.

2. **Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) – free plan**  
   - All‑in‑one: CRM, email marketing, and automation [5].  
   - Free plan sends up to 300 emails/day and includes basic sales automation [6][7].

Either one can work.  
If you want **one free tool that does a lot** (CRM + email + automation), **Brevo** is a strong choice.

---

### C. Automation engine: simple “if this then that”

You need something that says:

> “If a new lead signs up for X type of product, then do Y.”

You can get this in two ways:

1. **Built‑in automation in your CRM**
   - HubSpot has workflow and follow‑up tools, but deeper automation is on paid plans [3].
   - Brevo lets you **automate sales activities, deals, and tasks**, and multi‑step marketing for up to 2,000 contacts even on free/basic tiers [5][6].

2. **Standalone automation tool**
   - **Zapier**: connects 9,000+ apps and automates workflows between your site, CRM, email, etc. [8].  
     - Starter/pro plans are around \$19.99–\$29.99/month for 750+ tasks [9][10].
   - **n8n** (if you’re technical, can self‑host cheaply) [11][12].

For “best simple” and minimal tools, you can often stick with:

- **Brevo as all‑in‑one (site form + CRM + email + automation)**, or  
- **HubSpot Free + Zapier** if you prefer HubSpot’s interface.

---

## 3. Concrete automation workflows you should set up

Below are **four essential automations**, tailored to your product mix.

### Workflow 1 – Lead capture → contact in CRM

**Trigger:** New form submission on your website/landing page  
**Actions:**

1. Create a **Contact** in your CRM (HubSpot or Brevo) with:
   - Name, email, phone
   - Product interest (Tools / Software / Websites / Mining platform)
   - Source (e.g., “Website form”)
2. Create a **Deal/Opportunity** in a basic pipeline:
   - Stage: “New Lead”
   - Product type stored as a field

This can be done with:

- HubSpot or Brevo forms directly, **or**
- External form + Zapier (“new submission → create contact + deal”).

---

### Workflow 2 – Auto email sequence for small tools / software / websites

For buyers of **lower‑ticket, more digital products** (tools, software, websites):

**Trigger:** Contact created with Product Interest = “Tools” OR “Software” OR “Websites”

**Emails to automate:**

1. **Day 0 – Instant welcome**
   - Subject: “Here’s what you asked for”
   - Content:
     - Thank them
     - Short explanation of your solution
     - Link to product page or demo
2. **Day 1–2 – Show value**
   - A use case, feature highlight or quick tutorial.
3. **Day 3–4 – Proof**
   - Case study, testimonial, or before/after result.
4. **Day 5–7 – Offer**
   - Time‑limited bonus, discount or free add‑on for buying now.
5. **Day 10+ – Check‑in**
   - Quick email: “Still interested in X? Do you have any questions?”

Brevo’s automation can handle these multi‑step email sequences on its free/cheap plans [5][6].

---

### Workflow 3 – High‑touch follow‑up for mining & crushing platform

For the **mining & crushing platform** (high‑ticket, long sales cycle), automation’s job is to:

- capture interest
- send serious information
- get you into a call/demo

**Trigger:** Contact created with Product Interest = “Mining & crushing platform”

**Actions:**

1. **Immediate email (Day 0):**
   - Thank them for the inquiry.
   - Attach or link to:
     - Brochure / technical PDF
     - Capabilities and capacity specs
   - Clear next step: “Book a call/site assessment” (link to Calendly or CRM booking).

2. **Notify you or your sales rep:**
   - Auto‑create a **Task**: “Call this lead within 24 hours.”
   - Optionally send you an internal email or CRM notification.

3. **Reminder if no response:**
   - After 2–3 days, automatic follow‑up email:
     - Short: “Did you have a chance to review the information? Happy to discuss your current production and targets.”

4. **Pipeline stages for mining platform:**
   - New Inquiry
   - Needs Analysis (call done)
   - Proposal Sent
   - Negotiation
   - Won / Lost

CRMs aimed at heavy equipment dealers (like Nutshell or dedicated heavy equipment CRMs) are specifically built to manage long, complex deals, but your simple CRM can still model these stages [13][14].

---

### Workflow 4 – Automated reminders & basic lead scoring

**Across all products**, set up simple automations to avoid missed deals:

1. **No response in 7 days → Task**
   - If a deal stays in “New Lead” for >7 days:
     - Create a task: “Follow up with [Name] today.”
2. **Warm lead scoring (very simple)**
   - If a user opens 2+ emails or clicks a pricing link:
     - Tag as “Warm”
     - Move deal to “Engaged” or “Qualified”
3. **Abandoned form or partial info**
   - If your form captures email but they don’t finish checkout (for digital products):
     - Trigger an email: “Did something go wrong? Here’s the link again.”

Tools like HubSpot and Brevo support basic lead scoring and automatic task creation on higher tiers, and Zapier can implement simple rules if not available natively.

## 4. Example basic setup (one weekend)

### Day 1: Structure and tools

1. **Decide CRM**:
   - Want free and all‑in‑one? → **Brevo** [5][6].
   - Prefer HubSpot ecosystem? → **HubSpot Free CRM** [3].
2. **Create one simple landing page** with:
   - Clear headline: “Custom tools, software, websites & mining solutions”
   - Short description of each offer
   - One main form with “product interest” dropdown.

### Day 2: Automations

1. In your CRM, create **pipelines** (you can share one pipeline with a “Product type” field, or separate pipelines if needed).
2. Configure form → CRM automation:
   - On new form submission:
     - Create contact
     - Create deal in the right pipeline
3. Build **two sequences**:
   - **Sequence A**: for tools/software/websites (5–7 short emails).
   - **Sequence B**: for mining platform (3–4 serious, information‑heavy emails + call invite).
4. Turn on simple reminders:
   - Auto task after 3–7 days of no activity.

## 5. If you want “one platform and done”

If you are okay with paying **around \$97+/month** and learning one bigger system:

- **GoHighLevel**:
  - All‑in‑one CRM, pipeline, SMS, funnels, booking, automation [15][16].
  - Designed for agencies, but works for single businesses too.
  - You can:
    - Build funnels/landing pages for each product type
    - Do email + SMS follow‑ups
    - Automate pipeline movement

This is **not the cheapest**, and has more complexity than the Brevo/HubSpot approach, but it may be attractive if you want an “operating system” for your business in one subscription.

---

## 6. Clear, actionable recommendation

To keep it truly simple and still powerful:

1. **Pick Brevo as your core tool** (CRM + email + automation, free/cheap) [5][6].
2. **Build one landing page** with a form that has a “Product interest” dropdown.
3. **Set up 2 automation paths**:
   - Path A: tools/software/websites → auto email nurture + direct purchase links.
   - Path B: mining & crushing platform → immediate PDF/specs + invite to book a call + reminder tasks for you.
4. **Every week**, review your CRM:
   - Move deals through stages.
   - Call or email all “Warm” or “Needs Analysis” leads personally.

That gives you a **best simple automation** that:

- works for low‑ticket and high‑ticket offers
- doesn’t need custom code
- can start free or very low cost
- can be upgraded later (add Zapier, more advanced CRM, or GoHighLevel) without rebuilding everything.
